IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION NOTE
------------------------------------------------------------
The observed input gap index is a descriptive data-derived indicator.
It summarizes relative differences between districts in selected agricultural input/practice indicators.
It should not be interpreted as a causal prediction of agricultural failure.
